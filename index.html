About

Big companies have entire departments for this: continuous improvement teams, safety officers, industrial designers, procurement specialists, HR analysts. Small companies have an owner wearing every one of those hats. I've done this work inside world-class operations — aerospace electronics plants, tier-1 automotive at OEM pace, pharmaceutical projects under FDA scrutiny — and I bring the same methods to businesses of five to a hundred people. One person, many disciplines, no big-firm overhead. Engagements are practical and finite: we identify what's costing you the most, fix it, document it so it stays fixed, and I leave you with systems your team actually uses.
